Francis Ellis' Satirical Keyboard and Dry Humor at Punch Line Houston

Francis Ellis, a Freeport, Maine native, brings his dry, dark humor to Punch Line Houston on April 16, 2026. Known for his satirical keyboard songs, Ellis crafts performances filled with unexpected turns. This stop is part of his 'Francis Ellis Tour 2026'.

Ellis gained prominence after winning Barstool Sports' 'Barstool Idol' in 2017. His relaxed stage presence makes his storytelling both engaging and unpredictable. Fans of his YouTube special, "Gilly and Keeves: The Special", will find his live shows offer nuances and improvisational flair beyond the screen. His work in Netflix's "Tires" and the sketch "Blind Guy Ruins Wedding" on "Gilly and Keeves" highlights his commitment to character-driven comedy.

Ellis also co-hosts the 'Son of a Boy Dad' podcast, where his humor continues to evolve. In Houston, attendees can expect new material crafted specifically for this tour, delivering fresh insights with his trademark satirical edge. As noted on his official website, this tour promises surprises and new content.
