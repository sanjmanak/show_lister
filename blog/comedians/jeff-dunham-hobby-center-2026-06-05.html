Jeffrey Douglas Dunham's 'Artificial Intelligence' Tour Stops at Hobby Center Houston

"Silence! I kill you!" If that line doesn’t ring a bell, you’ve missed Jeff Dunham's peculiar world. Known for ventriloquism and stand-up, Dunham brings his 'Artificial Intelligence' tour to the Hobby Center in Houston on Friday, June 5, 2026, at 7:00 PM.

Since his first act in 1976, Dunham has carved a niche with puppets and punchlines. Specials like "Arguing with Myself" and "Spark of Insanity" on Comedy Central have become cultural touchstones. Characters like Walter and Achmed the Dead Terrorist deliver satirical punches to societal norms. Appearances on The Tonight Show and Late Show with David Letterman cemented his status as a household name.

Live, Dunham masters timing and improvisation, weaving current events into routines with puppets that seem alive. His humor, often skirting political correctness, finds its mark with audiences seeking laughs that challenge and entertain. The 'Artificial Intelligence' tour promises familiar irreverence with timely tech commentary.

The Hobby Center adds intimacy to his act. Beyond polished specials, there's unpredictability and rawness in live performance. The venue, nestled in Houston's heart, provides a fitting stage for Dunham's larger-than-life characters.

Dunham is also scheduled for a Mohegan Sun Arena performance later this year. Whether you're a long-time fan or new to his work, this show offers a chance to see one of comedy's enduring figures in action.
