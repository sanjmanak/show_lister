Bob Biggerstaff's Houston Return: A Night at The Secret Group

Bob Biggerstaff left a mark at Calgary's Comedy Cave, setting the stage for his return to Houston's The Secret Group. Known for self-deprecating humor and sports-infused storytelling, Biggerstaff honed his craft as a doorman at Houston's Laff Stop Comedy Club in 1997. Now, he's back in the city where it all began, with a comedic style both familiar and sharp.

From doorman to performer, Biggerstaff's journey shows dedication. By 2000, he was a regular on the comedy circuit, and in 2003, he claimed the title of Houston's Funniest Person. His TV appearances, including NBC's "Last Comic Standing" and Comedy Central's "Live at Gotham," have solidified his reputation.

His comedy, blending personal anecdotes and sports banter, fits The Secret Group's intimate setting. At $14.59–$16.90 a ticket, the August 20th show promises an evening where his observational humor shines.

Fans know him from podcasts like "Doug Loves Movies" and "The Bob and Tom Radio Show," where his mix of humility and wit, tinged with local pride, is evident.
