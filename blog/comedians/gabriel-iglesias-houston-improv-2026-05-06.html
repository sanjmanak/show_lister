Gabriel Iglesias' '1976 Tour: A Fluffy Celebration' at Houston Improv

"I got off the Ozempic, and immediately I gained back 20 pounds like that. It is scary how quick you regain the weight." Gabriel Iglesias' candid admission, reported by The Daily Beast, might sound like a punchline. The comedian, known for his storytelling and energetic stage presence, brings his humor to the Houston Improv on Wednesday, May 6, 2026, at 8:00 PM.

Iglesias, or "Fluffy," has built a career on personal anecdotes and cultural observations, often using sound effects and character voices. His recent special, "The Legend of Fluffy", explores Latin American culture, obesity, and pop culture with his trademark humor. Audiences have loved him since 1997.

Fans of his journey from "Hot and Fluffy" to Netflix's "Stadium Fluffy" will find this live performance more intimate. Iglesias' shows are known for engaging storytelling and personal connection. Expect his exploration of the "six levels of fatness" and perhaps a nod to his experiences with weight-loss drugs.

With a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, Iglesias celebrates his achievements with the "1976 Tour: A Fluffy Celebration." The tour blends the familiar with the new, delivered with his characteristic warmth and wit.
