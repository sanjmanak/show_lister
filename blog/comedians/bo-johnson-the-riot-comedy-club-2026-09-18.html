Bo Johnson's 'Two Sailboats' Sets Sail at The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Bo Johnson, Seattle’s understated humorist, released his first full-length special, "Two Sailboats", in 2025. He performs at The Riot Comedy Club in Houston on September 19, 2026.

Johnson has appeared on Comedy Central's "Kevin Hart Presents: Hart of the City" and the Just For Laughs festival's "LOL Live!" His work on Don't Tell Comedy Secret Sets highlights his knack for weaving personal narratives.

The Riot Comedy Club offers an intimate evening with Johnson. A $25 ticket buys a front-row seat to a nuanced performance that thrives live.
