Jeffrey Alden Dye Brings "Who the Bleep Is That?" Energy to The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe in Houston

Jeff Dye, known for his role on "Better Late Than Never," will take the stage at The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe in Houston on Friday, October 2, 2026, at 7:00 PM. Dye's comedy, full of personal anecdotes and pop culture riffs, retains the same energy that made him a finalist on "Last Comic Standing."

Currently hosting "Who the Bleep Is That?" on Fox, Dye thrives in live settings where intimacy and improvisation shine. This interaction has made him a stand-up favorite since 2007.

His 2022 YouTube special, "The Last Cowboy in LA," showcases Dye's storytelling skills, blending tales of relationships and misadventures with a unique humor style. Whether as a panelist on "The Masked Singer" or on a podcast like "TV Guidance Counselor," Dye's energetic delivery keeps audiences returning.
