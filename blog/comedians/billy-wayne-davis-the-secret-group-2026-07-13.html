Billy Wayne Davis Brings His Southern Wit to The Secret Group in Houston

In the dim corners of comedy clubs, a Tennessee drawl cuts through the chatter. Billy Wayne Davis, the voice behind it, redefines the Southern comic with his mix of observational humor and political commentary. Fresh from San Diego's Mic Drop Comedy, Davis hits The Secret Group in Houston on Monday, July 13, 2026, at 8:00 PM.

Davis's comedy walks the line between absurd and astute, honed through appearances on Conan and Last Comic Standing. His Hulu special, Testify, showcases his knack for turning mundane observations into critiques of the status quo.

The Secret Group, known for its eclectic programming and intimate setting, suits Davis's humor perfectly. His podcast appearances on WTF with Marc Maron and Behind the Bastards highlight his ability to connect through candid storytelling.

Fans of Squidbillies or his cameo in Borat 2 will find his live performance a deeper dive into a mind blending Southern charm with a progressive punch.
