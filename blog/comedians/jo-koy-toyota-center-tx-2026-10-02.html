Jo Koy's "Blue in the Face" Returns to Toyota Center - TX, Houston

Jo Koy, fresh from his Netflix special "Jo Koy: Blue in the Face," will perform at Houston's Toyota Center on October 2, 2026. The special, premiering globally in September, showcases Koy's Filipino-American heritage and personal stories. Known for his energetic performances, Koy's live shows deliver a dynamic experience.

The Tacoma native started his stand-up career in 1989, honing a style reminiscent of Eddie Murphy and Chris Rock. His comedy thrives on storytelling, mixing personal anecdotes with themes of everyday life, Asian American culture, and human sexuality.

Fans of "Chelsea Lately" or his Netflix special "Jo Koy: Comin' in Hot" will recognize his skill in turning personal anecdotes into universal truths. Appearances on "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno" and "Jimmy Kimmel Live!" highlight his knack for humor in both the mundane and profound.

The Toyota Center show promises intimacy and spontaneity unique to live comedy. Koy's stage presence hinges on audience interaction as much as the jokes, creating an engaging dialogue rather than a mere monologue.
