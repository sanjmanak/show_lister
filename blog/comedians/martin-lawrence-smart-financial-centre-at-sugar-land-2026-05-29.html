Martin Fitzgerald Lawrence's 'Y’all STILL Know What It Is!' Tour Hits Smart Financial Centre in Houston

Martin Lawrence has reinvented himself. The buzz around his transformation is palpable, with videos of his weight loss circulating online. As Parade noted, "Martin Lawrence is looking better than ever." Fans who’ve followed his journey from the 90s sitcom Martin to his latest tour, 'Y’all STILL Know What It Is!', agree.

On Friday, May 29, 2026, at 8:00 PM, Lawrence will bring his blend of observational and physical comedy to the Smart Financial Centre at Sugar Land in Houston. Known for vibrant storytelling and character-driven sketches, Lawrence weaves humor from African-American culture, human sexuality, and race relations. His style, often compared to Eddie Murphy, remains engaging and relevant.

Lawrence's career took off with the sitcom Martin, where he played Martin Payne from 1992 to 1997. On stage, his performances showcase his charisma and ability to connect with audiences through both scripted material and spontaneous interactions.

The Houston stop is part of a tour that has seen Lawrence revitalized. His recent activities, including the viral attention from his weight loss, have introduced him to a new generation of fans who missed his earlier work, like Martin Lawrence Live: Runteldat in 2002.
