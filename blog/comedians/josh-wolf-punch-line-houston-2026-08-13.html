Josh Wolf's 'Campfire Special' Sparks at Punch Line Houston

Josh Wolf's latest comedy special, The Campfire Special, brings stories to life without the need for marshmallows. Known for engaging storytelling, Wolf mixes comedy and music, creating a show that breaks from traditional stand-up. On Thursday, August 13, 2026, he sets up this metaphorical campfire at Punch Line Houston, promising laughter rooted in personal experiences.

Wolf's style shines in observational humor, drawn from the chaos of family life. His narratives often explore parenting absurdities—a theme resonating with anyone negotiating with a toddler. His 2019 special, Father of the Year, set the tone, refined with each performance.

Fans of Chelsea Handler know Wolf from Chelsea Lately, where his quick wit stood out in round table discussions. He hosted The Josh Wolf Show and charmed audiences on Shark After Dark. These credits highlight his knack for connecting with audiences, live or on screen.

The Houston show is part of Wolf's ongoing storytelling exploration, with new material planned for 2026. Punch Line Houston's intimate setting promises unscripted moments that make live comedy unique. Known for adapting to his audience, Wolf's performance will likely feel more like a conversation than a monologue.
