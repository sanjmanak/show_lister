Phil Rosenthal's Culinary Comedy Journey at 713 Music Hall Houston

Phil Rosenthal, creator of "Everybody Loves Raymond", has spent recent years sharing his food and travel adventures in the Netflix series "Somebody Feed Phil". As the show transitions to YouTube in 2027 under a Banijay Americas deal, Rosenthal brings his humor to Houston's 713 Music Hall on Sunday, April 26, 2026, at 7:00 PM.

Rosenthal's comedy blends family anecdotes, everyday observations, and cultural curiosities with a gentle touch. Known for relatable humor, his performances feel like a chat with an old friend, rich with insights about the universal love of food and travel.

Rosenthal expressed excitement about the move to YouTube, saying, "We are thrilled to announce that 'Somebody Feed Phil' will move to YouTube, where our message of family, friendship, food, travel, and laughs can reach the most people."

For fans of Rosenthal's TV work, his live show adds improvisation and the unpredictability of performing in front of a live audience. The familiar themes of family dynamics and culinary exploration will likely take center stage.
