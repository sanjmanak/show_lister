Why Donnell Rawlings' Houston Improv Show Is More Than "Ashy Larry"

“I'm rich, biaaaaatch!” The phrase echoes through Chappelle's Show, a testament to Donnell Rawlings' comedic touch. In April 2026, he defended the reworking of a Michael Jackson biopic to exclude abuse allegations, sparking conversations in entertainment circles.

Rawlings, a fixture since his early days on Chappelle's Show, has built a career on candid storytelling. Rooted in urban life and race relations, his comedy resonates with audiences seeking humor that cuts through noise. His latest Netflix special, Chappelle's Home Team: Donnell Rawlings - A New Day, showcases personal anecdotes woven with cultural commentary.

On Friday, July 31, 2026, Rawlings brings this blend to the Houston Improv at 7:30 PM. Known for engaging live performances, he transforms the stage into a space where spontaneity reigns. His shows are unpredictable, with Rawlings riffing off the crowd, a skill honed through years of podcast appearances, including WTF with Marc Maron.
