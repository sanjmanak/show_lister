Craig Gass Brings Celebrity Voices to The Secret Group in Houston

Craig Gass, known for his roles on "Sex and the City" and voice work on "Family Guy," has carved a niche in comedy. Growing up in a deaf family in the Bronx, he learned to communicate by mimicking TV voices. This unique start led to a career filled with spot-on impressions of Tracy Morgan, Adam Sandler, and Christopher Walken. His precision draws comparisons to legendary impressionist Rich Little.

Recently, Gass performed at The Comedy Arena, captivating audiences with storytelling and impressions. His live shows are known for spontaneity and engagement. At The Secret Group, expect a mix of polished routines and improvisational flair, all in Gass's signature style.

Tickets for the 5:00 PM show range from $31.9 to $49.21. The Secret Group offers an intimate venue, enhancing the connection between performer and audience, ideal for Gass's humor.
