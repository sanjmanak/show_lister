Peter Revello's Staten Island Wit Takes Center Stage at The Riot Comedy Club Houston

On a brisk November night in 2025, Peter Revello took The Tonight Show stage, his Staten Island accent cutting through the lights as he recounted tales of marriage and absurdities. This debut on Jimmy Fallon's couch highlighted his honest, blue-collar perspective.

Revello, a regular at New York's Comedy Cellar, brings his observational humor to Houston's The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's on April 10th. His set blends personal anecdotes with working-class sensibility, offering punchlines on married life and Staten Island's quirks, all through a New York lens.

Without a Netflix special or podcast, Revello's charm is in the raw intimacy of live shows. His co-production roles in 'Whatever!' and 'Trigger Play Comedy Show' suggest an improvisational talent that thrives with a live audience.

Tickets range from $28.36 to $190.55. As he heads to DC Improv soon after, this Houston stop is a rare chance to catch Revello's act in an intimate setting, where his stories of New York life promise to resonate with Texans.
