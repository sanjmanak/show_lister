Peter Revello's Staten Island Stories Light Up The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Peter Revello, a Staten Island native, recently caught national attention with his debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. His sharp observations and grounded humor marked a defining moment in his career.

This Friday, April 10, 2026, Houston's Riot Comedy Club at Rudyard's hosts Revello for an evening of observational humor. Known for personal anecdotes and a blue-collar perspective, Revello often returns to his Staten Island roots, marriage, and quirky daily life.

Revello's live shows are interactive, making the audience part of the narrative. Regulars at The Comedy Cellar in New York City know his knack for spontaneity and connection. As co-producer of "Whatever!" and "Trigger Play Comedy Show," he masters the nuances of live comedy.

Tickets for Revello's set at The Riot Comedy Club range from $28.36 to $190.55. According to We Own The Laughs, this performance promises laughs and insight into everyday life. For those who saw his Tonight Show appearance, it's a chance to see his full range in an intimate setting.
