Peter Revello's Staten Island Wit Takes Over The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Winning the Comedy Prize 2025 in Shreveport, Louisiana, underscores Peter Revello's knack for capturing life's absurdities. "Thank you to Prize Fest and all the other comics. This is really cool," he said with a hint of pride. On Friday, April 10, 2026, Revello will bring his humor to The Riot Comedy Club at Rudyard's in Houston.

Revello's comedy speaks to anyone who's felt like an outsider in their hometown. His Staten Island upbringing—"one of the few Staten Islanders not currently in a union," he jokes—colors his humor with personal anecdotes and working-class sensibilities. Whether discussing marriage or life's quirks, Revello's material feels both intimate and universal, earning him spots on Comedy Central.

Regular at The Comedy Cellar and co-producer of the "Whatever!" and "Trigger Play Comedy Show," Revello thrives on the unpredictable elements of live performance. His recent set at the New York Comedy Club in Stamford, CT, showcased his ability to pivot between rehearsed bits and spontaneous banter, promising an engaging Houston show.

Tickets for the 7:00 PM show range from $28.36 to $190.55. The Riot Comedy Club's intimate setting is perfect for Revello's Staten Island stories. Get Tickets
