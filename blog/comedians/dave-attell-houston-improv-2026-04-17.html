Dave Attell's Mischief and Mastery at Houston Improv: A Night to Remember

"I don't care if I lose my entire North Korean fanbase." Dave Attell's humor is bold and irreverent. On April 17, 2026, he brings this style to the Houston Improv for a night of edgy, crafted comedy.

Fresh off his Netflix special, Dave Attell: Hot Cross Buns, Attell tours with vigor. Critics at Decider noted how his "offensive" jokes reveal brilliance in their absurd delivery. This is the Attell paradox: raunchiness underpinned by precision.

Attell's live shows bring club-level intimacy to venues like Houston Improv. His quicksilver crowd work and rapid-fire delivery transform the space. Recent performances at the Palace Theatre and Cobb's Comedy Club show his ability to blend seasoned material with improvisation.

Fans of Insomniac with Dave Attell or Bumping Mics will see him in his element. Expect themes on American culture, sex, and self-deprecation, delivered with mischievous energy, as heard on The Joe Rogan Experience.

The show starts at 7:30 PM. Get Tickets while you can.
