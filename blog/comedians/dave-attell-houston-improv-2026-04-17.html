Dave Attell's 'Hot Cross Buns' at Houston Improv: A Night of Dark Humor

Dave Attell stomps through comedy with the clunky boots of a seasoned New Yorker. Recently, he took his unfiltered humor to Rivers Casino Philadelphia, leaving the audience in stitches. This April, Houston will get its dose of Attell’s unapologetic wit at the Houston Improv.

Attell’s career, which began in 1988, is a masterclass in dark humor and rapid-fire delivery. His HBO special, "Dave Attell: Captain Miserable", and Netflix outing, "Dave Attell: Hot Cross Buns", showcase his knack for turning the mundane into the absurd. Known for hosting "Insomniac with Dave Attell", his observational style and quick wit have been compared to Louis C.K. and Dave Chappelle.

Expect his Houston performance to be no different. Attell’s sets are a tapestry of non-sequiturs and razor-sharp observations on American culture, drug use, and religion. While his specials capture his essence, there’s an unpredictable energy to his live shows. The intimacy of venues like the Houston Improv allows for spontaneous interactions—Attell’s sharp retorts to audience interjections are a highlight, making each show unique.

Mark your calendars for Friday, April 17, 2026, at 7:30 PM. While pricing details are best checked directly with the venue, securing a ticket to see Attell live is a wise investment for any comedy enthusiast.
