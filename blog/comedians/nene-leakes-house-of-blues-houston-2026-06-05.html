NeNe Leakes at House of Blues Houston: Reality TV's Boldest Voice Takes the Stage

NeNe Leakes, known for her candid style on "The Real Housewives of Atlanta," steps into the comedy spotlight at the House of Blues Houston on Friday, June 5, 2026, at 7:00 PM. Her storytelling, laced with humor and drama, promises an engaging evening.

Leakes gained fame on "The Real Housewives of Atlanta" from 2008 to 2020 and transitioned into roles on "Glee" and "The New Normal." Her comedy draws from her life, offering insights and anecdotes unique to her experiences. Fans will recognize themes from her TV appearances in her live shows, where she shares personal and entertaining tales.

Recently, Leakes made headlines with her romance with fashion designer Nyonisela Sioh. As reported by TMZ, her brief relationship with attorney Arthur Horne III is over, paving the way for her to bring fresh perspectives on love and life. This mix of personal revelation and sharp observation keeps audiences returning.

Her live shows are energetic and engaging, reflecting her reality TV roots where audience connection is crucial. The House of Blues Houston offers an intimate setting for fans to experience her larger-than-life personality. This performance promises a raw, unedited experience.
