Shawn Gardini Brings His Comedy to The Gordy in Houston

Shawn Gardini has a knack for turning the mundane into the memorable. Whether he's dissecting the absurdities of everyday life or offering a fresh take on cultural norms, Gardini's comedy invites audiences to see the world through his distinct lens. On Friday, April 3, 2026, he'll bring that perspective to The Gordy in Houston, offering a night that promises to stay with you long after the laughter fades.

Gardini's comedic journey has been marked by a series of intriguing steps. He first caught wider attention with his appearances on Matt and Shane's Secret Podcast and Kill Tony in 2024. Additionally, his role as 'Son' on the sketch comedy series Gilly and Keeves in 2021 demonstrated his versatility.

His appearances on shows like The trASHE podcast reveal a penchant for exploring themes like societal expectations and personal identity.

Tickets for Gardini’s show range from $28.36 to $140.65, offering options for every budget. The Gordy, with its reputation for hosting top-tier acts, provides the perfect backdrop for Gardini’s nuanced brand of comedy.
