Nimesh S. Patel's 'With All Due Disrespect' Tour Comes to Houston Improv

Nimesh S. Patel thrives on the unexpected. His special, "Instant Karma" on Netflix, weaves cultural identity and social commentary into personal anecdotes, delivered with a dry confidence reminiscent of Chris Rock and Dave Chappelle. Patel brings this observational acumen to Houston Improv on Tuesday, April 21, 2026, as part of his 'With All Due Disrespect' tour.

Patel launched his stand-up career in 2009 and rose to prominence after being discovered by Chris Rock, leading to writing gigs for events like The Academy Awards. His journey from local clubs to major theaters showcases his unassailable joke writing and prolific output, as noted in LongIsland.com. His self-released YouTube specials, including "Lucky Lefty" and "Thank You China," have amassed millions of views.

Live, Patel's performances are known for sharp wit and engaging delivery, helping him transition seamlessly from comedy clubs to larger venues. His TV appearances on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" and "Late Night with Seth Meyers" highlight his comfort with both intimate and expansive audiences.

At Houston Improv, audiences can expect cultural observations and personal stories delivered with an incisive edge.
