Ilana Glazer's 'Human Magic' at 713 Music Hall: Houston's Upcoming Comedy Gem

“Whatever feels most terrifying, I want to go to there.” Ilana Glazer turns personal anecdotes into cultural insights with ease. Her stand-up special, "Human Magic" on Hulu, mixes urban life, feminism, and sexuality into humor that's both intimate and expansive.

From co-creating "Broad City" to her stand-up tour, Glazer's path is anything but linear. Her recent Dallas show at the Majestic Theater was a hit. She told KERA News, "I'm just so excited to be back on the road and connect with audiences in person again." Next stop: Houston's 713 Music Hall on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, at 7:00 PM.

Glazer's live shows are known for energy and engagement. Her performances focus on spontaneous audience connections, not just polished routines. Fans of "The Planet Is Burning" will recognize themes, but it's the candid moments that define her act.

At 713 Music Hall, expect urban tales and incisive observations delivered with humor and heart. Tickets are available through Ticketmaster. Get Tickets
