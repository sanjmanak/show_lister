Gary Owen's 'No Hard Feelings Tour' Expands to Houston Improv

Gary Owen extends his 'No Hard Feelings Tour' into Fall 2026, hitting over 30 new cities across North America. This expansion comes "due to popular demand," according to Icon Vs. Icon, underscoring the comedian's lasting appeal.

Owen's comedy mixes observational humor with fearless storytelling, drawing from his personal life and military background. His bold style first gained attention on BET's 'Comic View' in 1997. Since then, he's tackled various projects, including 'The Gary Owen Show,' which offered a peek into his family life.

Known for resonating with diverse audiences, Owen's live shows create a shared experience that feels both intimate and immediate. His performances blend well-honed bits with on-the-spot improvisation, ensuring each show is unique.

Born in Cincinnati, Ohio, Owen's journey from a six-year stint in the U.S. Navy to comedy stages is itself compelling. This background shapes much of his material, adding authenticity and relatability. The extended tour dates and packed venues speak to his enduring connection with audiences.
