Craig Robinson's Irreverent Comedy and Music at Houston Improv

Craig Robinson recently revealed a dramatic weight loss at the Critics Choice Awards, crediting "working out, intermittent fasting. It’s like the cheat code." This energy boost should spark his performance at the Houston Improv on Thursday, June 25, 2026.

Best known as Darryl Philbin on The Office, Robinson blends comedy with music, often backed by his band, The Nasty Delicious. His style is irreverent and socially conscious, weaving race relations and personal stories into his sets. Fans of Brooklyn Nine-Nine will recognize the sharp wit that made Doug Judy, the Pontiac Bandit, unforgettable.

Robinson's live shows are more than just laughs; they are a communal experience. His mix of stand-up and musical interludes creates a dynamic atmosphere. After leaving teaching for comedy in 1998, Robinson honed his craft, showing range in roles like Mr. Robot, but his roots remain in stand-up.

The Houston Improv, known for its intimate setting, offers an up-close encounter with Robinson's humor and musical talent. With his recent lifestyle changes, Robinson's performance might embody his own "cheat code" for infectious energy and entertainment.
