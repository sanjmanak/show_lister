Cristian Gutierrez Brings His Concrete Comedy to House of Blues Houston

Cristian Gutierrez, known on stage as Concrete, mixes laughter with community care. Hailing from San Fernando Valley, he's organized events for families with autism. This June, he brings his humor to the House of Blues in Houston.

Concrete's comedy draws from family life and Latino culture. The House of Blues Houston sets the stage for his honest style, where he's built a reputation in comedy clubs. A live show with Concrete means audience interaction and improvisation.

Gutierrez has a varied background, from towing trucks to accounting, before comedy. This journey fuels his material and his support for the neurodivergent community. His story of resilience was featured in LatiNation.

Concrete performs at the House of Blues Houston on Saturday, June 20, 2026, at 7:00 PM. The evening offers laughs and connection. Get Tickets
