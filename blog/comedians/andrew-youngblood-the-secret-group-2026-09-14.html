Andrew Youngblood's Dark Humor Illuminates The Secret Group in Houston

"February is my favorite month — Black History Month." This line captures Andrew Youngblood's flair for edgy humor. Originally from upstate New York, Youngblood now calls Texas home. His debut special, "I'll Tell You This", released on YouTube in 2024, showcases his skill in tackling race, sexuality, and politics.

Youngblood's punchlines mix hilarity with a hint of discomfort. At venues like The Secret Group, his performances crackle with a raw energy that live comedy uniquely offers.

His July 2025 show at Lafayette Comedy highlighted his evolving style. A 2023 podcast appearance on The Mess Hall revealed his comedic philosophy: challenge societal norms while keeping audiences engaged.

The Secret Group will host this free event, inviting anyone intrigued by Youngblood's dark comedy. It's a chance to see a comedian who blends laughter with introspection. Get Tickets.
