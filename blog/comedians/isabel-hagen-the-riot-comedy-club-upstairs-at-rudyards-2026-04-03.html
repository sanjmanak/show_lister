Isabel Hagen Brings Her Deadpan Wit to The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Isabel Hagen once quipped, “Canceling Disney+ was a bigger conversation than our engagement was.” This is the kind of sharp, existential humor you can anticipate when she takes the stage at The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's in Houston on Friday, April 3, 2026, at 7:00 PM. Hagen’s comedy might make you question your life choices while you’re still laughing at her deadpan delivery.

Hagen’s journey from Juilliard-trained violist to stand-up comedian is as intriguing as her punchlines. After a physical injury and performance anxiety disrupted her classical music career, she pivoted to comedy in 2015. Her unique blend of stand-up and viola performances has been captivating audiences since. Her television special, "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End", released in 2026, showcases her ability to merge these worlds seamlessly. Her appearances on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in March 2020 and October 2022 further cement her as a rising star in comedy.

Hagen’s comedy style is a delicate balance of deadpan delivery and existential musings. She crafts her sets with the precision of a classical musician, often weaving personal experiences and social awkwardness into her narratives. Her humor is reminiscent of comedians like Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin, yet she adds a layer of classical music, making her performances a singular experience.

Live, Hagen’s shows are an intimate experience. The Riot Comedy Club, known for its cozy setting, amplifies her nuanced performance style. Expect an evening where the boundaries between comedy and concert blur, with Hagen’s viola acting as both a punchline and a poignant pause. Her pacing is deliberate, allowing each joke to land with the weight of a well-played note. In Houston, with ticket prices ranging from $28.36 to $190.55, the audience will be treated to a performance that is as thought-provoking as it is entertaining.
