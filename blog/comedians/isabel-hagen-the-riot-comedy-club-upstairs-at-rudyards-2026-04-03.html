Isabel Hagen Brings Her Dry Wit to The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Isabel Hagen's comedy is a study in contrasts. The Juilliard-trained violist turned stand-up comedian doesn't just perform; she orchestrates her sets with the precision of a maestro, blending existential musings with the unexpected punch of a viola bow. On Friday, April 3, 2026, Houston's Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's will host this symphonic stand-up act, inviting audiences to experience her unique blend of humor and classical music.

Hagen's journey from the concert hall to the comedy club began in New York City, where she was born and raised. By age 10, she had already switched from violin to viola, a decision that would eventually lead her to Juilliard, earning both bachelor's and master's degrees. But it was the performance anxiety she developed there that nudged her toward stand-up in 2015. Her appearances on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in March 2020 and October 2022 are noteworthy.

Hagen's comedy style is both dry and existential, often drawing comparisons to the likes of Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin. Her routines are a tapestry of performance anxiety and life as a classical musician, woven together with the deftness of someone who has played with artists like Björk and Vampire Weekend. Her sets are structured not just with punchlines but with musical interludes, giving audiences an auditory palette cleanser before diving back into her sharp observations.

Live, Hagen's performances are intimate yet expansive. She seamlessly integrates her viola into her stand-up, creating a rhythm that ebbs and flows between comedic bits and musical pieces. The energy in the room is palpable, as she effortlessly commands attention with her deadpan delivery and poignant insights into the absurdities of the classical music world.

On April 3, audiences at The Riot Comedy Club will have the chance to experience this firsthand. With ticket prices ranging from $28.36 to $190.55, it's an opportunity to see a performer who has been making waves with her recent feature film, On a String, which premiered at the 2025 Tribeca Festival and won Best Screenplay in a U.S. Narrative Feature. The show starts at 7:00 PM.
