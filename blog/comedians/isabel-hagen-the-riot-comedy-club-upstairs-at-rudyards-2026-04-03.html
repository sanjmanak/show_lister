Isabel Hagen Brings Viola-Infused Comedy to The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

When Isabel Hagen takes the stage, she's not just delivering punchlines; she's orchestrating an experience. Her deadpan delivery might remind you of Mitch Hedberg, but with an existential twist that’s all her own. As a Juilliard-trained violist turned stand-up comedian, Hagen has mastered the art of blending classical music with comedy, turning performance anxiety into a compelling narrative that resonates with audiences. On April 3, 2026, she'll bring this intricate fusion to The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's in Houston, TX.

Hagen's story is one of transformation. Originally from New York City, she began her career in stand-up in 2015 while grappling with a repetitive stress injury during her master's program at Juilliard. Her journey took her from the concert halls, where she played with the likes of Björk and Vampire Weekend, to the comedy clubs where she found her voice. Her television special "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End," released in 2026, captures the essence of her evolution: a seamless blend of humor and music that challenges traditional stand-up forms.

Hagen's comedy style is a study in contrasts. Her deadpan tone and existential musings might initially disarm you, but they quickly draw you into a world where social awkwardness and lifelong regrets are fodder for both laughter and reflection. It's a space where anxiety isn't just a theme; it's a character in her narrative, given life through her viola. Her performances on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon"—both as a comedian and a musician—highlight her versatility and ability to connect with audiences through shared human experiences.

Live, Hagen's performances are a tapestry of timing and texture. The room fills with anticipation as she moves between stand-up and viola, each note and joke carefully orchestrated. Her crowd work is subtle, her pacing deliberate. The upstairs venue at Rudyard's, known for its intimate setting, will amplify her ability to engage directly with the audience, making each person feel like a part of her unfolding story. Expect an evening where the lines between music and comedy blur, creating a singular experience that stays with you long after the lights dim.

Catch this compelling blend of comedy and music at The Riot Comedy Club on Friday, April 3, 2026, at 7:00 PM. With ticket prices ranging from $28.36 to $190.55, there's an option for everyone looking to experience something truly different. Get Tickets
