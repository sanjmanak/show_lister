Isabel Hagen Live at The Riot Comedy Club, Houston

Isabel Hagen has a knack for turning life's anxieties into something almost symphonic. Imagine a Juilliard-trained violist who, after grappling with performance anxiety and injury, finds herself on the stand-up stage weaving existential musings with classical music. That's Hagen for you. Her bit about a more intense discussion around canceling Disney+ than her engagement encapsulates her sharp, dry humor—a blend that's earned her spots on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon and a recent TV special, "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End."

Hagen's journey from New York City's concert halls to the comedy clubs is as intriguing as it is inspiring. After her performance on "The Tonight Show" in March 2020, she captured attention for her ability to layer personal anecdotes with existential observations. Her 2026 special, aired on television, further cemented her status as a rising star. But it's not just her TV credits that matter; it's how she brings her viola into her routines, creating a unique comedic experience that stands out in the crowded stand-up scene.

Her comedy style is an intricate dance of timing and tone. Hagen's routines often play out like a symphony, her dry humor punctuating the rhythm. Recurring themes of performance anxiety and personal stories resonate with audiences who find solace in her vulnerability. Her existential humor draws comparisons to Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin, yet her incorporation of the viola sets her apart.

When Hagen takes the stage at The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's, expect an atmosphere charged with anticipation. The room's intimacy amplifies her ability to engage directly with the audience, blending crowd work with her structured sets. Her pacing is deliberate, allowing each joke to breathe, each note to linger. It's a performance that invites the audience into her world, a place where classical music and comedy coexist in harmony.

Mark your calendar for Friday, April 3, 2026, at 7:00 PM. Tickets range from $28.36 to $190.55, offering options for every comedy enthusiast. This isn't just another night out in Houston; it's an opportunity to experience a performer at the intersection of two artistic worlds.
