Isabel Hagen Takes the Stage at The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Isabel Hagen's comedy often begins with a viola in hand, but don't let that fool you into expecting a classical recital. Her deadpan delivery and existential musings serve as a reminder that life's awkward moments hold the most humor. As she quips, "I mean, canceling Disney+ was a bigger conversation than our engagement was." It's this kind of sharp, observational humor that has landed her on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon not once, but twice.

Hailing from New York City, Hagen transitioned from Juilliard-trained violist to stand-up comedian after a bout of performance anxiety and a physical injury. Since beginning her comedy career in 2015, she has seamlessly integrated her musical talents into her acts, crafting a unique niche that combines the precision of classical music with the spontaneity of stand-up. Her recent TV special, "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End", released in 2026, underscores her growing reputation as a comedian who defies easy categorization.

Comparisons to Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin hint at her mastery of timing and tone, but Hagen's style is distinctly her own. Her routines often explore themes of social awkwardness and personal experiences as a classical musician, delivered with a deadpan and existential edge.

Live, Hagen's shows are an intimate experience, where the audience is not just a passive observer but an essential part of the performance. The Riot Comedy Club, located upstairs at Rudyard's in Houston, offers the perfect setting for her brand of comedy. The venue's cozy atmosphere allows for a connection between performer and audience that is both immediate and engaging. Expect a night where viola interludes punctuate her sets, creating a rhythm that is as unpredictable as it is entertaining.
