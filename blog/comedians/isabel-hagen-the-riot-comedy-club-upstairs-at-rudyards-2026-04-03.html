Isabel Hagen Brings Her Viola-Infused Comedy to Houston's Riot Comedy Club

Isabel Hagen once quipped, "I mean, canceling Disney+ was a bigger conversation than our engagement was." This blend of sharp wit and relatable absurdity is quintessential Hagen. A Juilliard-trained violist turned stand-up comic, she delivers existential humor laced with raunchy anecdotes, a style that has resonated with audiences from "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" to comedy clubs across the country.

Hagen's journey from classical musician to comedian began in New York City, where performance anxiety and a physical injury nudged her toward the comedy stage in 2015. Her appearances on "The Tonight Show" in March 2020 and October 2022 established her as a fresh voice, blending the precision of a classical musician with the irreverence of a seasoned comic. Her recent TV special, "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End," released in January 2026, further cements her reputation for crafting narratives that explore anxiety and the quirks of life as a classical musician.

Hagen's comedy is an intricate dance of timing and tone, where existential musings meet the unexpected punch of raunchy humor. Her performances often integrate viola solos, offering a unique structure that defies the conventional stand-up routine. Drawing comparisons to the likes of Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin, her material frequently circles back to themes of anxiety and her experiences in the world of classical music.

When Hagen takes the stage at The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's on Friday, April 3, 2026, the audience can expect an experience that seamlessly marries music with comedy. Known for her engaging and dynamic live shows, she navigates her set with the ease of a conductor leading an orchestra, her viola both a prop and a partner in her act.

Tickets for the show range from $28.36 to $190.55. The show kicks off at 7:00 PM.
