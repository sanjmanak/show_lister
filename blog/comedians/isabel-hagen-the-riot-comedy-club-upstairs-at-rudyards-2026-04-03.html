Isabel Hagen Live at The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Isabel Hagen is no stranger to blending worlds. With a viola in hand and a mic in front of her, she navigates the existential with a dry wit that has charmed audiences across stages and screens. Her upcoming performance at The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's in Houston on Friday, April 3, 2026, promises an evening where classical music and comedy meet in unexpected harmony.

Hagen's journey from the hallowed halls of Juilliard to the stand-up stage is as compelling as her material. After earning her bachelor's and master's degrees in viola performance, she transitioned into comedy in 2015. This leap wasn't just a career shift but a seamless integration of her two passions. Her television special, "Isabel Hagen at the Bitter End", aired in 2026, showcases her signature style: a mix of stand-up and musical performances that reflect on themes like anxiety and her life as a classical musician. Her appearances on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in March 2020 and October 2022 cemented her reputation as a comedian who can hold an audience with both her punchlines and her bow strokes.

Hagen's comedy is often compared to the likes of Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin, yet she stands distinct with her ability to infuse classical music into her sets. Her tone is dry, her humor existential, and her delivery meticulous. She crafts narratives that ponder life's absurdities, often punctuated by a viola solo. Themes of anxiety and the peculiarities of the classical music world recur in her work, offering a window into her unique perspective.

Expect an intimate yet vibrant atmosphere when Hagen takes the stage at The Riot Comedy Club. Known for her engaging crowd work, she draws audiences in with a conversational style that feels both personal and universal. The venue’s intimate setting is perfect for her nuanced performance, allowing the audience to catch every smirk and subtle shift in tone. Her pacing is deliberate, each pause pregnant with anticipation, often leading to unexpected musical interludes.

The show kicks off at 7:00 PM, with tickets ranging from $28.36 to $190.55. It’s a fitting stage for Hagen, whose recent accolades include winning Best Screenplay for "On a String" at the 2025 Tribeca Festival. This performance is not just a chance to see a comedian but an opportunity to witness an artist seamlessly weaving two worlds into one.
