Zahid Dewji Returns to His Roots at Houston Improv: A Homecoming with Edge

In the chaos of New York Comedy Club's "Anxiety Attack," Zahid Dewji captivated the audience with each unexpected turn. His July performance showcased his knack for finding humor in unlikely places. Dewji will bring this skill back to his hometown at the Houston Improv on Wednesday, September 2, 2026, at 7:30 PM.

A Texas favorite since his early days, Dewji has a distinctive voice. Raised in Houston by East African Indian immigrants, his comedy blends cultural insights with personal anecdotes. His 2023 special, "Where is God?" on Don't Tell Comedy, reveals his philosophical yet irreverent approach.

With writing credits on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" and head-writing for the "Roast of Bert Kreischer" and "Roast of Whitney Cummings," Dewji's comedic pedigree is solid. These roles have honed his ability to craft jokes that provoke thought and laughter.

Dewji's live performances stand out for his ability to read the room and adjust material on the fly. This improvisational skill ensures no two shows are the same, making his Houston Improv gigs a unique experience.
