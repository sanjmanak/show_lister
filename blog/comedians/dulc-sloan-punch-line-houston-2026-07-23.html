Dulcé Sloan's Insightful Comedy at Punch Line Houston: A Night of Sharp Storytelling

Dulcé Sloan knows the resilience of storytelling. Her memoir, Hello, Friends: Stories of Dating, Destiny, and Day Jobs, released in 2024, chronicles her comedy journey with both joy and sorrow. This July, she brings her blend of personal storytelling and social commentary to Punch Line Houston, a venue perfect for her intimate humor.

Sloan's sharp voice was a staple on The Daily Show with Trevor Noah from 2017 to 2023, where she dissected cultural and social issues with wit. Her stand-up mirrors her TV work, offering keen observations on relationships and culture, much like Wanda Sykes. Live, Sloan's comedy shines, engaging audiences with candid critiques and punchlines.

Fans of her voice work as Honeybee Shaw–Tobin on The Great North will find her live show at Punch Line Houston electrifying. Her 2019 Comedy Central special showcased her talent for weaving humor with insight.

Catch Sloan in Houston on Thursday, July 23, 2026, at 7:30 PM. For tickets, check with the venue or Get Tickets.
