Matthew Broussard Brings His Heady Humor to The Gordy in Houston

Matthew Broussard is the kind of comedian who can make calculus feel like comedy gold. With a degree in computational and applied mathematics from Rice University, Broussard has the rare ability to weave complex concepts into his stand-up, turning physics and grammar into punchlines that resonate with the curious and the casual listener alike. His special "Hyperbolic," released on Amazon Prime Video in 2024, showcases this blend of intellect and humor, cementing his reputation as the thinking person's comic.

Broussard's comedic journey began in 2011, and his rise has been nothing short of impressive. After winning Houston's Funniest Person in 2012, he was featured as a New Face at the 2015 Just for Laughs Festival in Montréal. His 2016 Comedy Central special, "The Half Hour: Matthew Broussard," further established him as a formidable presence in the comedy scene. Appearances on "The Tonight Show" and "Conan" have brought his cerebral comedy to a wider audience, while guest spots on shows like "The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel" and "The Mindy Project" highlight his versatility.

On stage, Broussard's style is heady, self-effacing, and richly educational. His humor dances around topics as diverse as the intricacies of grammar and the mysteries of the universe, all while maintaining a playful, witty tone. Self-deprecation is a recurring theme, inviting audiences into his world with a disarming charm that makes even the most abstract ideas feel accessible. His performances are not just about laughs; they're a deep dive into the mind of a comic who sees the world through a unique lens.

When Broussard takes the stage at The Gordy in Houston on Friday, April 3, 2026, the audience can expect a performance that is both engaging and enlightening. Known for his interactive crowd work, he creates a dynamic atmosphere where each show feels tailored to the room. His pacing is deliberate, allowing each joke to land with precision and each story to unfold with care. Tickets range from $34.61 to $171.84, offering options for fans eager to experience his humor up close.
