Matthew Broussard Brings His Intellectual Wit to The Gordy in Houston

Matthew Broussard is the kind of comedian who can make you laugh while teaching you something new. His jokes often feel like a masterclass in both comedy and curiosity, a combination that has earned him appearances on The Tonight Show and Conan. On Friday, April 3, 2026, he will bring this unique blend of humor to The Gordy in Houston, offering audiences a chance to experience his cerebral yet accessible comedy live.

Born in New Jersey but raised in Atlanta, Broussard is no stranger to the Houston comedy scene. After earning a degree in computational and applied mathematics from Rice University, he pivoted from a career as a financial analyst to stand-up comedy in 2011. Just a year later, he was crowned Houston's Funniest Person. His Comedy Central special, The Half Hour: Matthew Broussard (2016), and his Amazon Prime Video special, Hyperbolic (2024), showcase his knack for blending academic insights with personal anecdotes. You can learn more about his journey in this Houston Public Media interview.

Broussard's comedy style is often described as heady, self-effacing, and educational. His material frequently dives into topics like physics and grammar, all while being grounded in his own life experiences. His appearances on podcasts like Daily Comedy News with Johnny Mac offer further insight into his thoughtful comedic process.

Seeing Broussard live is an experience that promises more than just laughs. His performances are engaging, with a pacing that keeps audiences on their toes. Known for his witty crowd work and the ability to weave spontaneous interaction into his set seamlessly, Broussard creates an atmosphere that feels both intimate and intellectually stimulating.

On April 3, The Gordy in Houston will open its doors at 8:00 PM to welcome Broussard and his fans. With tickets ranging from $34.61 to $171.84, this is an opportunity for comedy enthusiasts and curious minds alike to see a comedian who brings a bit of academia to the art of stand-up. For those interested in exploring Broussard's work further, his guest roles on shows like The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel and The Mindy Project are worth checking out, as are his stand-up performances available on streaming platforms.
