Matthew Broussard Brings His Intellectual Comedy to The Gordy in Houston

Matthew Broussard stands at the intersection of academia and humor, delivering jokes that make you laugh as much as they make you think. With a degree in computational and applied mathematics from Rice University, Broussard's comedy is as brainy as it is self-effacing. "I understand that before I even started talking most of you already didn’t like me, and that’s OK because ’80s movies have taught you not to trust someone with my hair and bone structure," he quips, setting the stage for a night where intellect and humility play equal roles.

Broussard's comedy career began in 2011 in Houston. Known for his Comedy Central special, "The Half Hour: Matthew Broussard", he has also graced the stages of The Tonight Show and Conan. His appearances on Jeff Ross Presents Roast Battle and guest roles on shows like The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel and The Mindy Project showcase his versatility.

Broussard's comedy style blends the cerebral with the everyday. His performances weave playful, witty humor with topics ranging from physics to personal anecdotes, often peppered with his signature self-deprecation.

Live, Broussard is known for engaging the crowd with a sharp, interactive style. His shows are a dynamic blend of structured routines and spontaneous moments. As he takes the stage at The Gordy on Friday, April 3, 2026, at 8:00 PM, audiences can expect an intellectually stimulating experience.

Tickets for this event range from $34.61 to $171.84. The Gordy, a venue known for its intimate setting and excellent acoustics, provides the perfect backdrop for Broussard's brand of comedy.
