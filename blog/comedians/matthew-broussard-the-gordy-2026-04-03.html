Matthew Broussard Takes the Stage at The Gordy in Houston

Picture this: a comedian with a math degree who can make you laugh about physics, grammar, and the enigma of human experiences. Matthew Broussard is that comic, and his heady, self-effacing humor is set to fill The Gordy in Houston on Friday, April 3, 2026, at 8:00 PM.

Broussard’s path from financial analyst to celebrated comedian isn't just a quirky backstory—it's a blueprint for his comedy. After snagging the title of Houston's Funniest Person in 2012, he became a New Face at the 2015 Just for Laughs Festival in Montréal. His specials, like “Hyperbolic” on Amazon Prime Video (2024) and his Comedy Central half-hour in 2016, showcase a comedian who isn't afraid to be both smart and vulnerable.

Broussard’s comedy style defies the typical punchline chase. It's a playful, witty exploration of complex themes. His performances are like a mental workout wrapped in humor, inviting audiences to laugh while pondering topics like mental health and personal growth. His stand-up appearances on “The Tonight Show” and “Conan” reveal a performer who is both precise and personable, balancing intellectual humor with a touch of the absurd.

Live, Broussard is a dynamic force. His crowd work is sharp, his pacing deliberate, making every moment feel intentional. The Gordy, known for its intimate atmosphere, will provide the perfect setting for his cerebral comedy. With ticket prices ranging from $34.61 to $171.84, the experience promises both value and insight.
