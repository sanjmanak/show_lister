Matthew Broussard Brings His Wit to The Gordy in Houston

Matthew Broussard once quipped, "I understand that before I even started talking most of you already didn’t like me, and that’s OK because ’80s movies have taught you not to trust someone with my hair and bone structure." It’s this brand of self-effacing, intellectual humor that has set him apart in the comedy world. On Friday, April 3, 2026, Broussard returns to Houston—a city pivotal in his comedic journey—to headline at The Gordy.

Raised in Atlanta, Broussard is a product of both academic rigor and comedic ambition. After earning his degree in computational and applied mathematics from Rice University, he ventured into stand-up, quickly making a mark by winning Houston's Funniest Person Competition in 2012. His work spans platforms, including his 2016 Comedy Central special, "The Half Hour," and his 2024 Amazon Prime Video special, "Hyperbolic".

Broussard's comedic style is a calculated blend of heady and self-effacing humor, often drawing on academic subjects and personal anecdotes. Known for his clever wordplay, he can weave a tale that feels both educational and entertaining. His performances are reminiscent of Bo Burnham's approach but with a distinct voice born from his own experiences and intellectual pursuits.

Live, Broussard is known for engaging performances that blend intellectual humor with personal storytelling. The Gordy's intimate setting will enhance this dynamic, offering a front-row seat to Broussard's cerebral yet accessible brand of comedy.

As you make plans for April 3, consider the tiered ticket pricing, ranging from $34.61 to $171.84, which reflects the diversity of seating options at The Gordy. The venue promises an evening where every seat feels like the best in the house, making it a prime spot for Broussard’s return to the city where his comedy career took off.
