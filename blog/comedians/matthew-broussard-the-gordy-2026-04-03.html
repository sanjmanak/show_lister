Matthew Broussard Brings His Edgy Wit to The Gordy in Houston

Matthew Broussard's comedy isn't just a set; it's an exploration. Known for weaving heady concepts with self-effacing storytelling, Broussard takes the stage at The Gordy in Houston on Friday, April 3, 2026, at 8:00 PM. He’s not just another comedian passing through town; he’s Houston's own, having clinched the title of Houston's Funniest Person back in 2012, a nod that launched his career from financial analyst to full-time humorist.

Broussard's journey is as intriguing as his punchlines. Born in New Jersey and raised in Atlanta, he pivoted from a degree in Applied Mathematics to the unpredictable world of stand-up. His cerebral yet accessible style is showcased in his specials, like "Hyperbolic," which began streaming in 2024, and "The Half Hour: Matthew Broussard" on Comedy Central in 2016. Each special layers his mathematical precision with a playful examination of topics ranging from physics to personal anecdotes.

On stage, Broussard is a master of structure and timing, delivering humor that is both educational and deeply personal. His comedy often meanders through the intricacies of grammar and the nuances of mental health. It's a style that has earned him spots on "The Tonight Show" and "Conan," where his stand-up performances drew wide acclaim. His competitive spirit shone through on "Jeff Ross Presents Roast Battle."

At a live show, expect Broussard to engage with the room like a seasoned conductor, orchestrating laughter with precision. His storytelling is intimate, almost as if he's revealing secrets to a room full of confidants. The venue's intimate setting amplifies his ability to connect directly with the crowd.

As the night unfolds, Broussard will likely navigate through topics both light and profound, blending his personal experiences with broader observations. The result is a performance that feels both spontaneous and meticulously crafted. With ticket prices ranging from $34.61 to $171.84, this evening promises a blend of humor and insight.
