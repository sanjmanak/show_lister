Graham Keay’s 'Pete & Me' Lands at The Riot Comedy Club, Houston

Graham Keay's latest special, 'Pete & Me', hits The Riot Comedy Club in Houston on Thursday, September 10, 2026. Known for his sharp observational humor, Keay explores the complexities of family dynamics, focusing on his relationship with his older brother Pete, who has profound autism.

An Ottawa native, Keay has carved a niche in comedy. His appearances on The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon and The Late Show with Stephen Colbert highlight his knack for turning personal experiences into relatable comedy. In 'Pete & Me', Keay blends stand-up with poignant anecdotes, offering insight into life with his brother.

His previous specials, 'Stupid Jokes' and 'Low Talking & Yelling', made him a regular at New York’s Comedy Cellar. The Huffington Post Comedy calls his style "quick, clever, deliciously sarcastic, and brilliantly funny." Houston's show promises intimacy and humor for just $15 at 7:00 PM.
