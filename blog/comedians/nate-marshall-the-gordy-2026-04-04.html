How Nate Marshall's Honest Comedy Unfolds at The Gordy in Houston

This is Nate Marshall, a comedian whose work draws on life's raw edges to craft humor that's both insightful and unflinchingly honest. His upcoming show at The Gordy in Houston on April 4, 2026, promises to be an evening where honesty collides with humor in unexpected ways.

Marshall has a knack for dissecting everyday occurrences—like dog-walking or the nuances of relationships—and turning them into poignant reflections on race and identity. His appearances on the sketch show Gilly and Keeves showcase his ability to blend humor with social commentary.

A frequent guest on podcasts like Matt and Shane’s Secret Podcast and Skeptic Tank, Marshall has honed his voice in spaces where conversation and comedy intersect.

The show is set to begin at 8:00 PM, with tickets priced between $28.36 and $40.84.
