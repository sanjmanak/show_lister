Nate Marshall Brings Honest Comedy to The Gordy in Houston

In a world where comedy often tiptoes around difficult topics, Nate Marshall dives headfirst into the deep end with a refreshing blend of honesty and wit. The Philadelphia-born comedian, who sharpened his craft as the House MC at the Reading Comedy Outlet, is set to perform at The Gordy in Houston on Saturday, April 4, 2026, at 8:00 PM.

Marshall's journey from Philadelphia to New York City has seen him grace stages from ArtsQuest to The Stand. As a co-host of the podcasts "Devil Tree" and "Panties In The Mouth", Marshall showcases his ability to weave humor into conversation, a skill he brings effortlessly to his live shows.

Marshall's comedy is honest and insightful. His material often gravitates towards themes of race, relationships, and even the peculiarities of dog-walking. His style isn't about delivering punchlines for laughs alone; it's about crafting a narrative that resonates.

The Gordy, known for its intimate atmosphere, provides the perfect backdrop for Marshall's brand of comedy. With tickets priced between $28.36 and $40.84, the evening promises to be an accessible opportunity to experience a comedian who is as thought-provoking as he is entertaining.
