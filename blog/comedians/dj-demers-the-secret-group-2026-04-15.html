D.J. Demers's 'A Few Loosies At Hecklers': Experience the Evolution at The Secret Group Houston

"I'm very easy to murder," D.J. Demers quips with deadpan delivery. This humor, steeped in personal anecdotes, defines his latest special, "A Few Loosies At Hecklers". Released in 2025, it captures his clever style, blending everyday challenges with his experiences of hearing loss. This unique perspective draws audiences to his live show at The Secret Group in Houston on Wednesday, April 15, 2026.

Performing since 2009, Demers has appeared on America's Got Talent and Conan, and starred in the CBC sitcom "One More Time." His routines often include sign language interpreters, adding intimacy and accessibility. This blend of personal experience and humor sets him apart, drawing comparisons to John Mulaney.

His show at The Secret Group promises more than a rehash of his specials. Known for engaging live performances, Demers thrives on the unpredictable nature of live comedy. Impromptu audience interactions and spontaneous riffs showcase his skill in creating shared experiences. Tickets for the 8:00 PM show range from $20.36 to $26.13.
