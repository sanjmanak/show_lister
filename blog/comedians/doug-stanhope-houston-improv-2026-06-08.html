Doug Stanhope's 'Beer Hall Putsch' Spirit Returns to Houston Improv

In 2024, Doug Stanhope launched the ‘Get to the Points Podcast’ with Brendon Walsh and Sean Green. Known for his sharp wit and fearless comedy, Stanhope continues to dissect American culture and politics. This podcast is his latest platform for challenging the status quo, a natural evolution for someone who's been skewering sacred cows since the '90s.

On Monday, June 8, 2026, Stanhope brings his unfiltered comedy to the Houston Improv at 7:30 PM. Known for his aggressive intelligence, Stanhope's performances are unpredictable and thought-provoking. His observational storytelling, laced with dark humor, leaves audiences laughing and rethinking perspectives on everything from religion to family dynamics.

Stanhope's career includes specials like 2013’s ‘Beer Hall Putsch’ and 2020’s ‘The Dying of a Last Breed.’ His stint co-hosting ‘The Man Show’ with Joe Rogan cemented his reputation as a provocateur. Yet, it's the live shows where he truly shines. The Houston Improv's intimacy allows for unscripted moments of brilliance, where Stanhope's razor-sharp observations cut through the noise.

His recent podcasting may hint at a more reflective phase, but Stanhope's core remains unchanged. The man who once raged at a preacher’s sermon during a city function still shakes things up, delivering comedy that challenges and entertains. To see Stanhope live is to engage with comedy that provokes thought and conversation.
