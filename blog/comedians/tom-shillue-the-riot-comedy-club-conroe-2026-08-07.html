Tom Shillue's Pathological Positivity Takes the Stage at The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe, Houston

"Woke is not dead." With a wry smile, Tom Shillue captures his 2026 comedy ethos. As a regular on Fox News' Gutfeld!, he navigates cultural minefields with a positive tone. His show at The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe in Houston on Friday, August 7, will mix cultural commentary with autobiographical storytelling.

Shillue's comedy is clean, engaging, and draws audiences into tales of fatherhood and personal experiences. His remarks on cancel culture, shared in a Fox News interview, show a performer aware of his landscape and ready to challenge it.

With a career that includes The Daily Show and hosting Red Eye, Shillue's versatility is clear. His time with "The Ragtime Gals" on Late Night with Jimmy Fallon highlighted his knack for harmonizing humor and music. Houston audiences can expect an evening of polished storytelling and spontaneous moments.

Tickets range from $34.61 to $227.97, reflecting varied seating at a venue known for its intimate atmosphere. For those interested in Shillue's unique mix of positivity and cultural insight, this performance offers a chance to see a comedian who balances humor and commentary without profanity.
