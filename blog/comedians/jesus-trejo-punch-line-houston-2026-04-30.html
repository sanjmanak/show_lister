Jesús Trejo's 'Roots of Comedy' at Punch Line Houston: A Night of Personal Narratives

In 2024, Jesús Trejo shifted gears from stand-up to host and produce the PBS series Roots of Comedy. This series, blending travelogue with social commentary, explored the origins of humor in communities. It sets the tone for Trejo's upcoming show at Punch Line Houston on Thursday, April 30, 2026, at 7:30 PM.

Known for narrative-driven sets, Trejo shares personal anecdotes reminiscent of Gabriel Iglesias. His specials, Stay At Home Son (Showtime, 2020) and Practicing (YouTube, 2023), tackle family dynamics and immigrant experiences. His relatable storytelling connects deeply with audiences.

Trejo's live shows are praised for their storytelling. His role as Mr. Trujillo on Mr. Iglesias and work on This Fool sharpened this skill. Appearances on The Late Late Show with James Corden and The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon highlight his ability to engage audiences with humor that's both personal and universal.

In Houston, the venue's intimacy will enhance Trejo's knack for improvisation. "I put together this hour and I'm happy that it's out," Trejo said. "I hope it resonates with people."
