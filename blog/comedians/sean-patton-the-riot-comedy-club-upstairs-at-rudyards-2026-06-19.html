Sean Patton's Storytelling Mastery at The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Sean Patton stands out in comedy by turning stories into unexpected laughter. He performs at The Riot Comedy Club upstairs at Rudyard's in Houston on Friday, June 19, 2026. With a new special set to be recorded in West Asheville later this month, the Houston show offers a peek into material that might make the final cut.

Patton, from New Orleans, brings Southern storytelling charm with a twist. His 2022 specials, "Number One" on Peacock and "King Scorpio" on YouTube, blend humor with introspection. On stage, he's known for taking chances, much like his appearances on Comedy Central's Live at Gotham and The Half Hour.

Houston audiences can expect an evening where the unexpected is the norm. Patton's live shows are known for intimacy and spontaneity, offering moments that specials can't capture. His previous stop at Madison's Comedy on State earlier this year set the stage for this, building anticipation for the Houston performance.

Join the audience at 7:00 PM, with tickets ranging from $28.36 to $190.55, catering to every comedy enthusiast. This is a rare chance to see Patton's storytelling prowess in a setting that embraces his adventurous spirit.
