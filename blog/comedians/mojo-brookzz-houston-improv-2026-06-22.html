Mojo Brookzz Brings 'Mr. James' to Houston Improv for One Night Only

Before hitting the Palazzo Theatre in Las Vegas this September, Mojo Brookzz stops at Houston Improv on Monday, June 22, 2026. Known for his high-energy style, Brookzz made waves with viral sketches that caught Nick Cannon's attention, landing him on Season 21 of Wild 'N Out. His role in Tyler Perry's Miss Governor on Netflix solidified his comedy credentials, but live shows reveal his true talent.

Dyon "Mojo" Brooks, a Chicago native, excels in blending sharp observational humor with storytelling. His themes—family, dating, social media—hit home with audiences. Characters like Mr. James (Sugardaddy) showcase his flair for impersonation and timing.

The Houston Improv show promises unpredictable moments and improvisational flair. Brookzz's past tours, including 'Unhand Me Now' and 'I Know You F*ckin' Lying', have set the stage for this performance. Houston audiences can expect a night of laughter and surprises. Whether impersonating his mom or sharing tales from his 'Thawt in My House' series, Brookzz engages and entertains.

The show starts at 7:30 PM. Get Tickets
