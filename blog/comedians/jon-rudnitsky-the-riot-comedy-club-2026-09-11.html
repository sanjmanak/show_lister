Jon Rudnitsky's Character-Driven Comedy Lights Up The Riot Comedy Club in Houston

Jon Rudnitsky elevates celebrity impressions beyond mimicry. His brief stint on Saturday Night Live left a mark with sketches like his "Dirty Dancing" bit on "Weekend Update." Now, he's set to bring his high-energy act to The Riot Comedy Club in Houston on September 11, 2026.

Rudnitsky's comedy blends observational humor with character-driven sketches. His style, akin to Andy Samberg, mixes pop culture parodies and precise celebrity impressions. Whether channeling a famous actor or dissecting everyday absurdities, Rudnitsky showcases sharp comedic timing and versatility.

Fans of his TV roles, like McWatt in the 2019 miniseries Catch-22 or Mike Devries in The Big Leap, will discover new facets of his talent live. His current projects, including filming Super Troopers 3, add timely relevance to his material.

Tickets are $20, making it an affordable chance to see a performer skilled in both comedy and drama. The Riot Comedy Club's intimate venue suits Rudnitsky's dynamic style perfectly.
