Adam Hunter's High-Energy Comedy Storms The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe in Houston

Adam Hunter delivers relentless punchlines with precision. Known for high-energy performances, he's set to bring his comedic whirlwind to The Riot Comedy Club — Conroe in Houston on Friday, July 24, 2026, at 7:30 PM.

A New York City native, Hunter blends marriage, fatherhood, pop culture, and politics into humor that resonates nationwide. Specials like "Throwing Punches" and "Adam Hunter's UFC Comedy Jam" highlight his knack for intertwining comedy and mixed martial arts.

At The Stress Factory Comedy Club in New Brunswick, New Jersey Stage called his act a "nonstop laugh" fest with lightning-fast crowd work. His improvisational style keeps shows fresh and unpredictable, something TV appearances on "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno" and "Chelsea Lately" can't fully capture.

As a finalist on Season 6 of "Last Comic Standing," Hunter gained national attention. His audience engagement, weaving responses into his set, creates a dynamic experience. The Riot Comedy Club's intimate setting will amplify this interaction.
