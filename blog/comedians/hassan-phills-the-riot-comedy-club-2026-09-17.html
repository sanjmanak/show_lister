Hassan Phills at The Riot Comedy Club: Houston's Chance to See Canada's Rising Star

In February 2025, Hassan Phills appeared on the outro of "Small Town Fame," a nod from Toronto's musical titan. Phills, a Scarborough native, brings his storytelling and spontaneous crowd interaction to The Riot Comedy Club in Houston on Thursday, September 17, 2026, at 9:00 PM.

Starting his comedy journey in 2015, Phills has become known for weaving his Jamaican and Djiboutian heritage and Toronto upbringing into his sets. As a main character on Crave's "The Office Movers", he established himself on both stage and screen. His live shows, however, truly capture his ability to make each performance feel like a conversation among friends.

In Houston, expect Phills to explore multiculturalism, identity, and religion, with a focus on Toronto culture. His engaging, interactive style often breaks the fourth wall, inviting the audience into his world. This reputation for memorable, intimate experiences has sold out theaters across North America.

Phills's world tour, "This Tour Is Jokes," visited 44 cities, each night showcasing his connection with diverse audiences. His performance at The Riot Comedy Club is a chance for Houston fans to see why he's one of Canada's fastest-rising comedians.

Tickets for Hassan Phills's Houston show are priced at $20. Get Tickets while they last.
