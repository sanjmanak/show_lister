John Crist's 'Emotional Support' Lands at Cullen Performance Hall in Houston

John Crist's comedy captures the chaos of "every parent at Disney." Known for his sharp takes on Christian culture and millennial woes, Crist brings his latest insights to Houston's Cullen Performance Hall on Saturday, April 18, 2026.

Crist's latest special, "Emotional Support", has already hit over 3.9 million views on YouTube. Raised in Lilburn, Georgia, as one of eight siblings in a pastor’s household, his humor offers an insider’s view on Christian subculture.

His style blends stand-up and sketch comedy, tackling themes like consumerism and family dynamics. Whether through "Millennial International: Sponsor a Millennial Today" or the viral "Weather Man MELTS down on live TV," Crist’s comedy enlarges everyday quirks.

Crist ranks among Pollstar’s Top 10 Global Touring Comedians, but his live shows stand out for their intimacy. The unpredictability of a live audience lets his humor evolve in real-time, offering improvisation that’s impossible on screen. Attendees at Cullen Performance Hall can expect familiar narratives turned hilariously unexpected.

The show starts at 7:00 PM. Confirm pricing with the venue, but tickets are available through Ticketmaster.
