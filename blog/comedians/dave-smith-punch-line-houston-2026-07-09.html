Dave Smith's Libertarian Wit Takes Center Stage at Punch Line Houston

“But in a comedy club, funny is the job, and he delivered at Friday’s opening show at Comedy Bar in the North Hills,” noted the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette after Dave Smith's March performance. Known for his keen political insights wrapped in biting humor, Smith returns to the stage this July at Punch Line Houston, promising a night where libertarian politics collide with comedy.

Smith's 2017 special, Libertas, captured his knack for critiquing government and media with precision. He has since become a familiar voice on podcasts like The Joe Rogan Experience and Timcast. His television appearances on Kennedy and The Greg Gutfeld Show further cement his place as a commentator who wields humor like a well-aimed dart.

At Punch Line Houston on Thursday, July 9, 2026, Smith will take the stage at 7:30 PM. Known for blending sharp political insight with humor, Smith's live performances are a masterclass in timing and delivery. Expect the unexpected as he navigates the current political landscape, perhaps touching on themes echoed in his notable bit: “Bush spent a whole year making his case. Trump just tweets at 2 in the morning — like, ‘Done!’”
