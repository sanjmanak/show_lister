Affion Crockett's Impressions and Energy at Houston Improv

Few comedians blend physical comedy with sharp impersonations like Affion Crockett. Since 1995, the Fayetteville, North Carolina native has carved out a unique niche, drawing comparisons to Jamie Foxx with his mix of sketches and stand-up.

Crockett’s style is a vivid mix of celebrity impersonations and hip-hop culture, reflecting his time on MTV's Wild 'n Out. His mimicry is more than a party trick; it's a vehicle for cultural critique wrapped in laughter.

His 2011 sketch show, In the Flow with Affion Crockett, showcased his versatility. Though brief, it confirmed his ability to helm a project, blending impressions with original sketches. Fans of Def Comedy Jam and Black-ish will find his live shows uniquely unpredictable.

Tickets for the 7:30 PM show at the Houston Improv are $31.4. Crockett’s performances are known for spontaneity and audience interaction, making the crowd an integral part of the act.
