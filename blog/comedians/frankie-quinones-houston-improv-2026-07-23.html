Frankie Quiñones' Hulu Hit 'Damn That’s Crazy' Comes Alive at Houston Improv

Few comedians channel the energy of sketch shows like In Living Color into solo acts as effectively as Frankie Quiñones. On July 23, 2026, Houston Improv hosts this Los Angeles native, whose Hulu special, "Damn That’s Crazy", stands out in the comedy scene. Quiñones sheds his characters to perform solo, a challenge he meets with the vigor defining his career.

Quiñones is familiar to viewers from Hulu's This Fool and FX's What We Do in the Shadows. His character-driven style turns stand-up into a one-man variety show. His viral Creeper’s CholoFit exercise routines blend cultural commentary with physical comedy, resonating with audiences familiar with Chicano culture.

On stage, Quiñones offers more than his specials' polished material. His performances, filled with improvisational moments, keep audiences engaged. At Houston Improv, expect a night where family dynamics and fitness parodies collide, delivered with his signature seamlessness.

In Houston, this show is a chance to see a comedian who turns sketches into viral hits and explores new comedic territories. The show starts at 7:30 PM. For pricing, check the venue site.
