LeMaire Lee Brings His Stand-Up to Rudyard's in Houston

LeMaire Lee isn’t just another name floating in the comedy ether; he's a voice honed from the gritty streets of Detroit to the electric stages of Austin. On Saturday, April 4, 2026, Houston's own Rudyard's will host Lee at 11:00 PM. Tickets range from $28.36 to $140.65.

Lee's comedic journey began in the vibrant city of Philadelphia, where he built a reputation for his incisive wit and sharp storytelling. His work on the Panties In The Mouth podcast with co-hosts Andy Malafarina and Nate Marshall has solidified his standing in the comedy community. As a producer on Matt and Shane’s Secret Podcast, Lee has further demonstrated his knack for crafting compelling, humorous narratives, a skill he brings to every live performance.

Lee's appearances on shows like the Real Ass Podcast reveal a comedian who thrives on spontaneity. His material often touches on the complexities of identity and culture.

Witnessing Lee live is an experience that goes beyond the mere act of attending a comedy show. His recent performances, such as those at Phantom Power in Millersville, PA, and his hosting stints at the 'Optimum Noctis' comedy series in Austin, showcase his ability to engage and energize a crowd. Each set is a dynamic blend of rehearsed material and impromptu interactions, ensuring no two shows are ever alike.

Rudyard's, known for its welcoming atmosphere and dedication to live performances, provides the ideal backdrop for Lee’s brand of comedy.
