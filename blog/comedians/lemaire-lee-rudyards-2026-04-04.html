LeMaire Lee Brings Detroit Humor to Houston at Rudyard's

Born in Detroit, Lee's comedic journey began as a way to navigate the lively, laughter-filled environment of his family. Now based in Austin, Texas, he has honed his craft in the bustling comedy scene of Philadelphia, where he quickly gained a reputation as a rising star. His work on the podcast "Panties in the Mouth," co-hosted with Andy Malafarina and Nate Marshall, showcases his ability to riff on everyday absurdities—a skill that translates effortlessly to the stage.

Lee's television work, particularly his roles in the sketches "Blind Guy Ruins Wedding" and "Militia Funeral" on "Gilly and Keeves," highlights his knack for character-driven comedy.

On this particular Saturday night, comedy enthusiasts can expect a set that reflects Lee's recent momentum, including his performance at Phantom Power in Millersville, PA. The show at Rudyard's, starting at 11:00 PM, promises to be a highlight of Houston's comedy calendar. Tickets range from $28.36 to $140.65, offering options for every type of comedy fan.
