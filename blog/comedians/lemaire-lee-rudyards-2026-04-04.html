LeMaire Lee Brings His Unfiltered Wit to Rudyard's in Houston

LeMaire Lee's comedy is an open book, and every page is pure, unvarnished honesty. This Detroit native, now making waves from Austin, has honed his craft not just as a performer but as a storyteller who pulls no punches. On April 4, 2026, Lee takes the stage at Rudyard's, bringing with him the raw energy that has earned him spots on podcasts like Real Ass Podcast and Sky King's Mental Playground.

Born in Detroit, Lee's comedic journey began in Philadelphia, where he quickly emerged as a promising talent. He's not just a stand-up comedian; he's a writer and creator who co-hosts the 'Panties in the Mouth' podcast alongside Andy Malafarina and Nate Marshall. His work as a producer on 'Matt and Shane’s Secret Podcast' adds another layer to his multifaceted career.

Lee's appearances on Kill Tony and 'Legion of Skanks' highlight his ability to engage with a diverse range of comedic voices.

On Saturday, April 4, 2026, at 11:00 PM, Rudyard's in Houston will transform into a comedy haven as Lee takes the stage. Tickets range from $28.36 to $140.65, offering a range of options for those eager to experience his unfiltered humor firsthand. Whether you're a long-time fan or new to his work, this performance promises an evening that is as enlightening as it is entertaining.
