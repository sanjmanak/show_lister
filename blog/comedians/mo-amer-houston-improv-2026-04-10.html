Mo Amer's 'Wild World' Returns to Houston Improv

When Mo Amer took the stage at the Riyadh Comedy Festival in September 2025, it drew international attention and criticism from Human Rights Watch. His ability to navigate controversial waters with humor and insight is a hallmark of his career.

Amer, a Houston native, brings his comedic voice back to the Houston Improv on April 10, 2026. Known for his observational style, Amer blends personal anecdotes with social commentary. His Netflix specials, Mo Amer: The Vagabond and Mo Amer: Mohammed in Texas, showcase a comedian adept at weaving stories both personal and universally resonant.

On television, Amer captivates audiences with roles in Ramy and his creation, Mo, portraying a Palestinian refugee in Houston. This duality of identity—refugee and Houstonian—permeates his comedy, offering fresh perspectives on Islamophobia and immigration. His recent special, Mo Amer: Wild World, continues this exploration with sharp humor.

Amer’s live performances are known for engagement and spontaneity, offering audiences a chance to experience the improvisational flair that sets him apart. This upcoming show at the Houston Improv promises a night where Amer’s storytelling shines, delivering laughter and reflection on cultural identity and political satire.
