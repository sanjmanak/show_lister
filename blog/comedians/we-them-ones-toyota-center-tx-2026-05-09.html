Mike Epps and the "We Them Ones" Comedy Tour

"We Them Ones Comedy Tour" is a comedic relay with Mike Epps at the helm. Known for his film and stand-up work, Epps anchors the show with his storytelling.

Tony T. Roberts hosts with rapid-fire delivery and unpredictable punchlines, weaving between sets with improvisation and crowd work.

The lineup includes Chico Bean, DC Young Fly, and Karlous Miller, veterans of "Wild 'N Out," known for freestyle improv and sharp social commentary. Mojo Brookzz and TK Kirkland add humor ranging from absurd to acerbic.

The tour is celebrated for collaborative segments where comedians riff off each other's material, creating a competitive yet camaraderie-driven dynamic.

At Houston's Toyota Center, the tour's ambition matches the venue's scale, promising an evening where stand-up boundaries are pushed.
