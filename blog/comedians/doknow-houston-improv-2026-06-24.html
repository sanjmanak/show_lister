Daniel Lopez's Echo Park Humor Takes the Stage at Houston Improv

DoKnow, born Daniel Lopez, has carved out a niche in social media comedy with short-form videos capturing life's chaos. Raised in Echo Park, Los Angeles, his comedy zeroes in on the relatable and absurd. His chat with Jerry Garcia on Cali 93.9 about their Perritos World National Tour cemented his blend of humor and candid self-reflection.

Lopez's comedy dives into everyday experiences, honed through videos and stand-up. His themes revolve around relationships and self-reflection, resonating with audiences who find solace in shared imperfections. On What's Up Fool? with Felipe Esparza, he showcased humor that's raw and accessible.

On June 24, 2026, Lopez will bring his voice to the Houston Improv. Known for selling out shows, his live performances are a testament to his dynamic stage presence. The Houston show promises spontaneous moments not captured on screen.

For those in Houston seeking comedy that feels personal and universal, this is a chance to see DoKnow in his element. The show starts at 7:30 PM. Get Tickets.
