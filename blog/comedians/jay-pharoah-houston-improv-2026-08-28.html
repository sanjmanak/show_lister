Jay Pharoah's Impressions Take Center Stage at Houston Improv

Jay Pharoah brings his arsenal of celebrity impressions to the Houston Improv on Friday, August 28, 2026. Known for his work on Saturday Night Live, Pharoah has moved from sketch comedy to hosting Fox’s The Quiz with Balls.

Pharoah's comedy mixes observational humor with impressions, drawing comparisons to Eddie Murphy. His Showtime special, Jay Pharoah: Can I Be Me?, tackled race, pop culture, and politics. Live, his energetic performances showcase his rapid-fire transformations.

The Houston Improv offers an intimate venue for Pharoah's improvisational skills and audience interactions. Inspired by Gilbert Gottfried's Iago, Pharoah began performing at 15 and has turned celebrity portrayals into a comedic art form.

Expect insights on current events and pop culture, with nods to his TV roles, including A Million Little Things. The show starts at 7:30 PM. For tickets, visit the Houston Improv ticket page.
