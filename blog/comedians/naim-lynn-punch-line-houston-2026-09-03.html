Na'im Lynn's Punch Line Houston Show: From Plastic Cup Boyz to Personal Insights

"You have to choose to be happy." Na'im Lynn has woven this simple philosophy through his comedy with the precision of a seasoned storyteller. Known for vibrant energy and personal anecdotes, Lynn has carved out a niche that's both familiar and incisive. On Thursday, September 3, 2026, he brings this humor to Punch Line Houston, promising laughter grounded in the everyday.

Since his days at Temple University, where he swapped a potential career in physical therapy for stand-up, Lynn has become a prominent figure in comedy. As a key member of Kevin Hart's Plastic Cup Boyz, he showcased his observational humor in the Netflix special "Laughing My Mask Off!" His podcast, "DMC w/ Na’im Lynn," recently featured a conversation with Comedian CP about ambition and fatherhood, mirroring his own stage themes.

On TV, Lynn has shown range, guest-starring as Dirty Paul on "Survivor's Remorse" and leading as Jeremy in "Tyler Perry's Assisted Living." These roles highlight his comedic timing and character depth, skills that translate to his live performances. At Punch Line Houston, expect energetic and engaging delivery, his hallmark.

Tickets range from $34 to $48, promising a showcase of Lynn's evolution as a comedian. The intimate venue offers a close-up view of his signature style—personal, observational, and relatable.
