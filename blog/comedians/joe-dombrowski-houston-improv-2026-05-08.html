Joe Dombrowski's 'Dad on Arrival' Lands at Houston Improv

“During the day I’m dealing with 5-year-olds who act like drunk adults and at night I’m dealing with drunk adults who act like 5-year-olds,” Joe Dombrowski quips. This blend of chaos fuels his comedy special, "Dad on Arrival", now streaming on YouTube. Produced by 800 Pound Gorilla Media, the special marks a new chapter where fatherhood takes center stage.

On Friday, May 8, 2026, the Detroit native brings his humor to the Houston Improv for a 6:30 PM show. Known for his observational style, Dombrowski mixes storytelling with personal anecdotes. His comedy, reminiscent of Jim Gaffigan, navigates marriage, family, and his former life as an elementary school teacher.

Dombrowski's rise from viral sensation began with a 2017 April Fools' Day prank that caught The Ellen DeGeneres Show's attention. Since then, he's appeared on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert and released two YouTube specials, including 2025's "Don't Eat the Crayons." His humor draws from the mundane, engaging live audiences with performances that feel intimate and improvisational.

In live settings, Dombrowski's interactive approach sets him apart, often weaving audience responses into his routines. The Houston Improv offers a chance to experience these spontaneous moments firsthand.
