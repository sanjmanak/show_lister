Houston, Nikki Glaser’s Hulu special *Good Girl* is a good reason to pay attention.

She has been doing stand-up since 2002, and her comedy is personal, candid, and willing to go where most people stop. Dating, getting older, sobriety, and the stuff people usually keep to themselves—she says it out loud. If you like comedy that feels like an honest conversation with fewer filters, I’d see this one.
