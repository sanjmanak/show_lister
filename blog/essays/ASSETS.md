# Comedian Essay Series — Reusable Asset Kit

Companion assets for the six essays in this directory. Each essay ships with:
pull-quotes (for quote-card graphics), a graphic concept (hand to a designer or
build in the existing Puppeteer pipeline), and an IG caption in the looser
social voice. Series color/type should match site brand: bg `#0a0a0f`, card
`#1a1a26`, accent `#ff4d6a`, secondary `#7c5cff`, Inter.

Publish cadence: one per week, Wednesdays 10am CT (see `config/essays.json`).
